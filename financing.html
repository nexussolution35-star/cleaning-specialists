How Our Financing Works

We've partnered with established, regulated lenders so you can fund your kitchen the easy way. There's no pressure to finance — many customers pay outright — but if spreading the cost helps, here's how simple it is.

1. Get your fixed quote

Start with a free in-home consultation and 3D design. Once you're happy with the plan, we lock the price in writing.

2. Choose a plan that fits

Pick from interest-free or low-rate longer-term options. We lay out every figure — monthly payment, total cost, term — before you commit. No hidden fees, no surprises.

3. Quick, paperless approval

Most applications get a decision in minutes. Once approved, we schedule your build and you start enjoying your kitchen.

Payment Options At a Glance

  • Pay in full — settle on completion, the traditional way.
  • 0% interest-free — spread over 12 months on qualifying projects.
  • Low-rate finance — longer terms for larger renovations.
  • Stage payments — pay as the project reaches agreed milestones.

Talk To Us About Financing

Finance is subject to status and affordability checks, provided by regulated third-party lenders. Terms, rates and availability vary by project. Ask us for full details during your consultation.
