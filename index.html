Questions

Frequently Asked

How fast can you quote my kitchen?
We usually visit within a few days of your call. You'll have your 3D design and fixed, itemised quote within a week of that visit — no high-pressure "today only" pricing, ever.
Do you handle design and permits?
Yes. We produce the full 3D design in-house and manage any permits, electrical certificates and plumbing sign-offs your project needs, so you're never chasing trades or paperwork.
Are you licensed and insured?
Fully. We're licensed and insured, and our electrical and plumbing work is completed and certified by qualified, registered tradespeople on our own team.
Do you use your own craftsmen or subcontractors?
Our own. The team that designs and quotes your kitchen is the team that builds it. That's how we keep quality consistent and the same friendly faces in your home from day one to handover.
What warranty do I get?
Every kitchen carries our written workmanship warranty on top of the manufacturer warranties on cabinets, worktops and appliances. One point of contact if you ever need us back.
